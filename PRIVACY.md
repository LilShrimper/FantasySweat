# Fantasy Sweat — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 10, 2026_

Fantasy Sweat is a Chrome extension that shows your fantasy football matchups and which NFL players to cheer for or against.

## What the extension stores

Everything below is saved only in your own browser (Chrome's extension storage) and is never sent to the developer:

- The Sleeper username you enter (optional)
- The ESPN league IDs you add and the team you pick in each
- League nicknames and display preferences (selected tab, filter, selected leagues, popout window size)
- Cached public data (the NFL player list and recent plays) so the extension loads quickly

You can remove all of it at any time by removing the extension or clearing its data.

## What the extension sends, and to whom

To show your matchups, the extension requests public data directly from:

- **Sleeper** (`api.sleeper.app`, `api.sleeper.com`, `sleeper.com`): your public Sleeper user, leagues, rosters, matchups, projections, stats and play-by-play, looked up by the username you enter
- **ESPN** (`lm-api-reads.fantasy.espn.com`, `site.api.espn.com`): the public ESPN leagues you add, plus the NFL schedule and live scores

These requests go straight from your browser to Sleeper and ESPN and are covered by their privacy policies. The extension does not ask for or read your Sleeper or ESPN password, cookies or login.

## What the extension does not do

- No accounts, sign-ups or logins
- No analytics, tracking, ads or third-party scripts
- No selling or sharing of any data
- No data is sent to the developer or any server other than Sleeper's and ESPN's
